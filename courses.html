About our courses

More than lessons — a path to transformation.

At Dalswin Life & Business Institute, our courses transcend mere lessons. They represent a movement that began in 2007 with a fundamental mission: to make life-changing education accessible to all.

Our programmes are carefully crafted not only to impart knowledge, but to foster personal and professional development for individuals from varied backgrounds. By merging theoretical frameworks with experiential learning, we ensure that participants not only grasp the concepts, but can implement them effectively in their everyday lives.

Whether you are a young adult in pursuit of direction, a professional looking to refine your leadership skills, or an individual simply seeking clarity and personal growth, our programmes are designed around your needs. Each person’s journey is unique — and our courses are built to empower you in yours.

Class 07

Interpersonal Harmony & Conflict Resolution

Regulate the nervous system mid-conflict and negotiate lasting resolutions.

Course overview

Interpersonal conflict is not merely an intellectual disagreement; it is a somatic crisis. When humans experience conflict, the brain perceives a threat to social belonging, triggering the same Sympathetic-Adrenal-Medullary (SAM) axis that responds to physical predators. Unresolved, chronic relationship conflict acts as a constant “leaking faucet” of cortisol and adrenaline, contributing directly to the 30% stroke risk and systemic depressive pathologies that plague modern society. This course equips students with the scientific knowledge and practical behavioral skills required to intercept the conflict loop, regulate the autonomic nervous system mid-crisis, and negotiate mutually beneficial resolutions.

Key learning outcomes

  • Deconstruct: Identify the physiological and neurological markers of the acute conflict response.
  • IBR Framework: Apply the Interest-Based Relational framework to separate people from structural problems.
  • Conflict Dynamics: Calculate emotional escalation patterns using the Conflict Dynamics Formula.
  • Under Pressure: Execute active-listening and nonviolent communication (NVC) protocols under high stress.
  • Mediation: Mediate disputes within families, professional teams, and local communities as certified Life Transformers.

Class 08

Counselling Techniques & Narrative Guidance

Blend clinical frameworks with narrative work to guide real transformation.

Course overview

The counseling and intervention curriculum is built on the philosophy that therapeutic intervention is a precise, clinical, and bio-behavioral science, rather than just an abstract, conversational art. The program is designed to equip advanced students with foundational counseling skills that blend traditional counseling methods with principles related to spiritual growth and inner transformation. It prepares helpers to address both the emotional and mental challenges of a client, as well as their deeper search for meaning, connection, and purpose.

Key learning outcomes

  • Integrate Clinical Frameworks: Balance person-centered empathy with evidence-based strategies from CBT, DBT, and somatic psychophysiology.
  • Decode the Nervous System: Accurately read underlying autonomic nervous system indicators and decode a client’s spoken words.
  • Manage Crises Safely: Use non-violent intervention and de-escalation skills to handle high-stress client interactions ethically.
  • Reconstruct Personal Narratives: Guide clients in deconstructing defective core beliefs and rewriting their personal stories.
  • Somatic Down-Regulation: Prioritise trust and lower nervous system arousal to create the safety needed for deep emotional work.

Class 09 · New programme

Computer Literacy & Workplace Digital Skills

From first click to workplace-ready — a practical four-week programme in Windows, Microsoft Office, email, the internet and everyday AI tools.

Course overview

This programme prepares learners for offices, businesses, schools and organisations where computers are used every day. It goes beyond switching on a computer: learners practise the tasks employers expect — operating Windows, writing and formatting professional documents, building spreadsheets, preparing presentations, communicating by email, researching online and using AI tools responsibly. Teaching is 30% theory and 70% practical, and the course ends with a simulated office assignment. Excel receives particular emphasis because so many workplace roles depend on accurate records, stock, attendance and reporting.

Key learning outcomes

  • Windows confidence: Start up, navigate, organise files and use keyboard shortcuts independently.
  • Professional documents: Type, format, lay out and export letters and reports in Microsoft Word.
  • Working spreadsheets: Capture data, apply formulas, sort and filter, and build charts in Excel.
  • Clear presentations: Design and deliver a professional PowerPoint presentation.
  • Digital communication: Write professional emails, manage attachments, use cloud storage and join online meetings.
  • Workplace readiness: Handle data entry, registers, invoices and reports with confidentiality.
  • Responsible AI: Use AI assistants to draft, summarise and solve problems — then verify and edit the output.

How our courses work

A steady rhythm for every course.

The eight core courses are divided into weekly learning units with written and practical exercises — usually three assignment tasks per week. The Computer Literacy programme follows a four-week, five-day timetable with daily practical work and weekly assessments.

Weekly Learning Guide

Your instructor posts weekly tasks. The guide contains the reading material, assignment questions and instructions for each unit.

Written assignment

Task one of the week: a structured written task that helps you think deeply and apply the unit’s concepts.

Discussion forum

Task two of the week: exchange perspectives with fellow learners and learn from the experience of others.

Journal assignment

Task three of the week: reflect personally on what you are learning and how it applies to your life.

Who we are

An education that reaches beyond the classroom.

Dalswin Life & Business Institute is a domain and trading name for Dalswin Investments, registered in Zimbabwe under the Companies Act [Chapter 24:03]. Since 2007, we have empowered individuals with the knowledge, skills and support they need to live a life of purpose, success and personal growth.

We believe education is not a privilege — it is a right. Our free and paid courses in life management, business, personal development, leadership and emotional intelligence are designed to be accessible, affordable and immediately useful.

We are more than an educational platform. We are a community of dreamers, doers and changemakers.

How core courses work

A simple, steady weekly rhythm

Our eight core courses are delivered online, so you can learn around the rest of your life. The Computer Literacy programme follows its own four-week, five-day timetable.

1

Enrol and choose your course

Create your account and choose a programme through the student portal.

2

Study your weekly Learning Guide

Your instructor posts weekly tasks. The Learning Guide contains the reading material, assignment questions and instructions for each unit.

3

Complete your three assignments

Submit a written assignment, a discussion forum assignment and a journal assignment for every unit.

Every week, every unit

Each learning unit follows a clear structure, so your progress is steady, practical and measurable.

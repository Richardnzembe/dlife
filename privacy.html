Introduction

Welcome to Dalswin Life & Business Institute. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our website, services, and courses.

By accessing our website, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

Information we collect

We may collect personal information such as:

  • Your name, email address, and contact details when you register for courses, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us.
  • Course participation data, feedback, and progress.
  • Non-personal information such as browser type, device, and general location for analytics purposes.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide access to courses, content, and services.

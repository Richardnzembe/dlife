How to enrol

Four simple steps to begin

From your first sign-in to your first learning unit, the process is designed to be straightforward.

1

Create your account

Visit the student portal and create your account, or sign in if you are a returning learner.

2

Choose your course

Select one of our nine courses. If you are unsure where to begin, contact us for guidance before enrolling.

3

Access your Learning Guide

Your instructor posts weekly tasks. The Learning Guide contains the readings, assignment questions and instructions for each unit.

4

Complete your assignments

Submit a written assignment, a discussion forum assignment and a journal assignment for every unit.

Admissions FAQ

Questions before you enrol?

Here are the answers to the questions we hear most often.

Are all courses free?

Some courses are free, while others require payment. Pricing is clearly indicated for each course. You can review the programmes on our Courses page or in the student portal.

How is each course structured?

Our eight core courses are divided into eight weekly learning units with written and practical exercises. Unless your instructor instructs otherwise, each learning week carries three assignment tasks. The Computer Literacy & Workplace Digital Skills programme instead runs for four weeks, with daily practical sessions and weekly assessments.

What assignments will I complete?

In the core courses, each week includes a written assignment, a discussion forum assignment and a journal assignment. Your Learning Guide contains the reading material, questions and instructions for the unit. The Computer Literacy programme is assessed through weekly practical tasks and a final office project instead.

Do I need to be in Zimbabwe to study?

No. Our courses are delivered online and serve learners in Zimbabwe and beyond. All you need is internet access and the willingness to invest in your growth.

How do I access my class?

After enrolment you will be given a class invitation link or access code. Classes, learning guides and submissions are accessed through the student portal.

What is the refund policy for paid courses?

Payments for paid courses are final and non-refundable unless otherwise stated in writing. Please review our Terms and Conditions, or contact us before enrolling if you have any questions.

Who can I contact for help?

Email us at info@dlife.online. We are happy to help with course selection, portal access and any other questions you may have.

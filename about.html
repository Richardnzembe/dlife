Who we are

Education for a life of purpose, success and growth.

Dalswin Life & Business Institute is a domain and trading name for Dalswin Investments, officially registered in Zimbabwe under the Companies Act [Chapter 24:03]. Since our founding in 2007, we have been dedicated to empowering individuals with the knowledge, skills and support they need to live a life of purpose, success and personal growth.

At Dalswin, we understand that life can be overwhelming without the right guidance and tools. That is why we offer a wide range of free and paid courses in life management, business skills, personal development, leadership and emotional intelligence. Our programmes help you navigate challenges, unlock your potential and become a better version of yourself — whether you are just starting your journey or looking to sharpen your skills for the next chapter.

We believe that education is not just a privilege — it is a right for everyone. Our courses are accessible and affordable without compromising on quality, and our diploma programmes deliver high-value, practical learning at a fraction of the cost of other qualifications.

Dalswin is more than an educational platform. We are a community of learners who believe in the power of knowledge to transform lives and shape a brighter future for all.

What guides us

Mission, approach and promise

Everything we teach is grounded in a belief that education should inform, equip and transform.

Our mission

To equip a global community of lifelong learners with the emotional, psychological and spiritual intelligence required to live a life of deep impact and authentic fulfilment.

Our approach

We merge theoretical frameworks with experiential learning, mentorship and community engagement — so that knowledge becomes practice in everyday life.

Our promise

Accessible, affordable, high-value education. Our diploma programmes are offered at a fraction of the cost of other qualifications, without compromising on quality.

What sets our courses apart

Practical education, built around people

Our founders identified a gap in traditional education: people need more than theory. They need actionable insight, practical experience and the support to apply both.

Practical learning

We favour practical learning over theory that never leaves the page. Hands-on activities, discussion and personal reflection turn concepts into skills you can use.

Affordability & accessibility

We believe everyone, regardless of economic status or physical ability, should have access to essential learning without substantial financial or practical obstacles.

Community & mentorship

Content alone is not enough. Collaboration, mentorship and a nurturing community help knowledge become skill — and relationships become lasting support.

Transformative education

We believe in the power of education to change lives — encouraging learners to pursue their passions, realise their potential and contribute to their communities.

Our legacy

Rooted in our legacy. Committed to your future.

Since our inception, we have assisted numerous individuals in Zimbabwe and beyond in taking decisive steps toward a more promising future through a methodical approach to education and personal development.

Our courses are instructed and overseen by dedicated professionals who embody Dalswin’s original vision — education that transforms the whole person. Each programme is crafted not only to deliver knowledge, but to cultivate critical thinking, creativity and emotional intelligence.

We are resolutely committed to empowering our learners, guiding them along a pathway of self-discovery and growth, and enabling them to envision and shape a more luminous future for themselves and their communities.
